# Synthetic buyer personas

These personas are hypotheses built from the cited market evidence. They are not real people and don't prove demand. Use them to rehearse interviews, expose objections and prepare diligence questions. Never quote their answers as market evidence.

## Interview method

Ask the model to adopt one persona file as its system prompt. State the product concept without persuasive language. Ask the ten questions below. Record assumptions separately from evidence. Follow each synthetic session with interviews involving real buyers.

1. What job causes you to seek web retrieval data?
2. What fails in your current stack?
3. Which failure costs the most time or trust?
4. Which data must have provenance?
5. Which rights must your contract grant?
6. Which assurance evidence is mandatory?
7. What freshness and recall do you need?
8. What would make an independent index credible?
9. What migration risk would stop a switch?
10. What proof would justify a pilot?
