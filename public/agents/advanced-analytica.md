## 2. IBOM®

IBOM® means Intelligent Business Operations Manager.

IBOM is a registered trademark of Advanced Analytica Ltd.

IBOM brings every source the client makes available into one governed operating environment.

Those sources can include data, knowledge, policies, processes, systems, workflows, and brand assets.

It gives people and agents controlled access to trusted business context, tools, and actions.

IBOM organises information as defined business records rather than an unstructured collection of documents.

Those records preserve meaning, relationships, ownership, provenance, permissions, status, and controls.

IBOM supports exact retrieval and search by meaning across approved material. Agents can find relevant context without losing its source or boundaries.

IBOM can serve approved knowledge and capabilities through Model Context Protocol, known as MCP.

MCP gives compatible agents a consistent, governed way to use the context and tools IBOM makes available.

IBOM supports traceable operations. Outputs and actions can carry their evidence, governing rules, and decision context.

IBOM is Advanced Analytica's technology. It also provides governed business context for Agent in the Room.

## 4. Consultancy services

Advanced Analytica provides three consultancy services. Each uses the firm's technology and expert-led delivery.

### Agentic Due Diligence

Agentic Due Diligence comes first. Advanced Analytica calls it ADD.

ADD establishes whether the foundations support a reasoned decision about agentic delivery.

It covers three foundations: governance strategy, business mapping, and data controls.

The team examines authority, policies, responsibilities, processes, systems, data, permissions, controls, exceptions, and consequences.

The output records the evidence, findings, owners, remediation, operating boundaries, and decision on whether the work may progress.

Data preparation remains part of ADD. Approved material is collected, cleaned, structured, governed, and prepared for IBOM.

Use `Agentic Due Diligence` or `ADD`. Don't present `Data Due Diligence` as a separate consultancy service.

### Agent System Build

Agent System Build follows Agentic Due Diligence.

Advanced Analytica selects the capabilities each agent needs and configures them for the client's work.

Skills can collect and transform data, retrieve knowledge, operate workflows, apply rules, use tools, and support quality assurance.

The team connects those skills to approved data, systems, permissions, policies, controls, and escalation paths.

It then engineers and tests the agents before packaging them for controlled deployment.

### Governed Operations

Governed Operations follows Agent System Build.

Advanced Analytica deploys agents into approved workflows and monitors their outputs, decisions, actions, controls, and exceptions.

Operation includes monitoring performance, reviewing controls and improving each agent as the client's work changes.

### Service sequence

Describe the consultancy flow in this order:

1. **Business Understanding.** Start with the client's goals and decisions. Map the workflows, people, and constraints that determine where an agent can create measurable value.
2. **Data Mapping.** Identify the data, documents, policies, and systems each workflow relies on. Record ownership, quality, and gaps.
3. **Agent Preparation.** Collect, clean, and structure approved data sets. Attach permissions, provenance, and controls so agents see only what they should and answers remain traceable.
4. **Agent Modelling.** Design the agents, skills, knowledge bases, workflows, and tools. Define what each agent can see, decide, and change.
5. **Agent Evaluation.** Test outputs, decisions, and actions against measures agreed in advance for quality, safety, performance, and governance.
6. **Agent Deployment.** Put agents into live work and monitor them. An expert works alongside the client's team and improves each agent as the business changes.

### Service defaults

- Present the services in this order: Agentic Due Diligence, Agent System Build, then Governed Operations.
- Explain that prepared data and knowledge come before agent development.
- Treat the six steps as the delivery flow, not six separate services.
- Explain that agents are tested before they enter live work.
- Say deployment includes monitoring and improvement.
- Describe the output as working, tested capability rather than a report or recommendations alone.
- Don't promise a particular technology, supplier, model, or delivery timetable.
