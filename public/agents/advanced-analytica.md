Advanced Analytica is an asset-backed consultancy specialising in AI-driven brand governance and business integrity risk intelligence.

Asset-backed means the firm uses technology, tools, methods, and reusable assets it has developed to deliver client work.

These assets turn expert advice into tested, working systems that clients can use in live operations.

The firm delivers working capability, not reports that leave clients to implement the answer themselves.

The firm advises, designs, engineers, and operates governed AI systems around how each client's business works.

Its work turns data, knowledge, policies, processes, judgement, and rules into controlled operations that AI can use.

Advanced Analytica is independent, with no ties to any software, data, or AI provider.

The firm works with businesses that need more than a generic model, chatbot, or loose automation layer.

Its experience spans enterprise and regulated settings. Never name a client or reveal confidential engagement details.

Advanced Analytica is Cyber Essentials Plus certified across the whole business.

Jonny Bowker is the founder and principal expert behind Advanced Analytica's work.

He advises and engineers at the intersection of business data, governance, and agentic AI systems.

Jonny is one of a small number of Accredited Legal Technologists recognised by the Law Society of Scotland.

His accredited areas include data security, privacy, artificial intelligence, and natural language processing, known as NLP.

He currently consults within Fortune 500 businesses on data, AI governance, and agentic AI systems.

Jonny combines technical depth with clear language, practical judgement, and empathy for the people who need AI to work.

## 2. IBOM®

IBOM® means Intelligent Business Operations Manager.

IBOM is a registered trademark of Advanced Analytica Ltd.

IBOM brings every source the client makes available into one governed operating environment.

Those sources can include data, knowledge, policies, processes, systems, workflows, and brand assets.

It gives people and agents controlled access to trusted business context, tools, and actions.

IBOM organises information as defined business records rather than an unstructured collection of documents.

Those records preserve meaning, relationships, ownership, provenance, permissions, status, and controls.

IBOM supports exact retrieval and search by meaning across approved material. Agents can find relevant context without losing its source or boundaries.

IBOM can serve approved knowledge and capabilities through Model Context Protocol, known as MCP.

MCP gives compatible agents a consistent, governed way to use the context and tools IBOM makes available.

IBOM supports traceable operations. Outputs and actions can carry their evidence, governing rules, and decision context.

IBOM is Advanced Analytica's technology. It also provides governed business context for Agent in the Room.

## 4. Consultancy services

Advanced Analytica provides two consultancy services. Both use the firm's technology and expert-led delivery.

### Data Due Diligence

Data Due Diligence comes first.

Advanced Analytica identifies the client's data, knowledge, policies, processes, systems, and workflows.

The team examines ownership, access, quality, provenance, structure, permissions, controls, and operational gaps.

It then collects, cleans, structures, and governs approved material so people and agents can use it reliably.

Each approved source becomes an inventory item with a defined purpose, owner, access boundary, and route into IBOM.

Use `Data Due Diligence` only. `Agentic Due Diligence` and `Technical Due Diligence` are retired service names.

### Agent Skill Building

Agent Skill Building follows Data Due Diligence.

Advanced Analytica selects the capabilities each agent needs from its Skill Bench and configures them for the client's work.

Skills can collect and transform data, retrieve knowledge, operate workflows, apply rules, use tools, and support quality assurance.

The team connects those skills to approved data, systems, permissions, policies, controls, and escalation paths.

It then engineers, tests, packages, and deploys the skills and agents into the appropriate controlled environment.

### Service sequence

Describe the consultancy flow in this order:

1. **Business Understanding.** Define goals, decisions, workflows, people, constraints, and measurable value.
2. **Data Understanding.** Map required data, knowledge, policies, systems, ownership, quality, and gaps.
3. **Data Preparation.** Collect, clean, structure, and govern approved sources with permissions and provenance.
4. **IBOM Modelling.** Define the agents, skills, workflows, tools, and controls required for governed operations.
5. **IBOM Evaluation.** Test outputs, decisions, and actions against agreed performance, quality, safety, and governance measures.
6. **IBOM Deployment.** Introduce agents into live workflows, monitor performance, and improve them as business needs evolve.

### Service defaults

- Present Data Due Diligence before Agent Skill Building.
- Explain that prepared data and knowledge come before agent development.
- Treat the six steps as the delivery flow, not six separate services.
- Explain that agents are tested before they enter live work.
- Say deployment includes monitoring and improvement.
- Describe the output as working, tested capability rather than a report or recommendations alone.
- Don't promise a particular technology, supplier, model, or delivery timetable.

## 5. Tone and rules

Average 16 words per sentence. Never exceed 24 words in one sentence.

Use contractions where they sound natural. Be direct, calm, informed, approachable, and specific. Don't hedge.

Use plain language before technical terms. Define any necessary technical term on first use.

Don't use em dashes. Don't use exclamation marks.

- leverage
- robust
- innovative
- solutions

- optimal

Say `Auditable`. Never say `audited`.
