# High-volume content needs governed approval before it goes live

This case study shows how a global travel organisation can approve high-volume content against brand and legal rules before publication. Travel content creates a demanding approval environment because campaigns, offers, destination copy, partner material, and regulated claims all move quickly across markets. The agent converts brand and legal requirements into structured checks, returns clear pass or fail decisions, and records the audit trail needed to explain why a piece of content was approved, rejected, or sent for further review.

## Challenge
- Every piece of content had to be checked before publication.
- Brand and legal rules were detailed, overlapping, and difficult to apply consistently.
- Manual teams could not review every submission at the required pace.
- The organisation needed a governed decision process, not a simple content scoring tool.

## Approach
- Converted brand guidelines and legal requirements into structured rules.
- Defined the checks required for each type of content submission.
- Built an agent that ingests submitted content, runs sequential compliance checks, and returns a pass or fail decision.
- Attached the rule basis to each decision so reviewers could inspect the reason behind any rejection.
- Created an operating model for updating rules as brand and legal standards changed.

## Outcome
- Content is approved or rejected automatically before it reaches publication.
- Review teams focus on exceptions rather than routine rule checking.
- The business gains a consistent decision trail across channels and markets.
- Brand and legal teams can show how rules were applied at the point of approval.

## Real-world example

A global travel business needed content checked against brand and legal standards at a volume manual reviewers could not sustain. We built an agent that applies the rules and produces a governed decision for each submission.
