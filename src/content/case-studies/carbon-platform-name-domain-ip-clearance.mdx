# Platform naming is faster when legal and creative work together

This case study shows how a carbon settlement platform can develop positioning, naming, domain architecture, and IP clearance in one coordinated workflow. The work reflects the reality that creative naming decisions and legal screening often move at different speeds, creating late-stage risk when names, domains, and marks are already embedded in product and investor materials. By running brand strategy, domain hierarchy, and conflict screening together, the team could make faster decisions, reduce rework, and keep the Strand and repay.earth positioning aligned to commercial and natural capital market requirements.

## Challenge
- A new carbon settlement platform needed a name, domain structure, and trademark clearance.
- Creative development could not wait for a slow legal sequencing process.
- Provisional names risked late-stage conflict and rework.
- The platform needed decisions captured as they locked so downstream work stayed aligned.

## Approach
- Created a structured brief covering positioning, naming constraints, domain logic, and IP risk criteria.
- Ran name generation, domain schema design, and conflict screening in parallel.
- Captured candidate status, conflicts, availability, and decision rationale in one working register.
- Locked decisions as evidence was confirmed so creative development could continue against approved assets.
- Kept naming, domain, and creative strategy aligned through one decision workflow.

## Outcome
- The platform received a cleared name, coherent domain hierarchy, and usable creative platform together.
- Late conflict risk was reduced because screening ran in real time with creative work.
- The team avoided rework caused by rejected marks or unavailable domains.
- Decisions were documented in a form the business could review and maintain.

## Real-world example

A carbon settlement platform needed brand assets cleared while creative work continued. We ran naming, domain design, and IP conflict screening together so the creative platform developed against confirmed assets.
