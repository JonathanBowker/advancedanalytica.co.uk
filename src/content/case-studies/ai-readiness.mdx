# AI Readiness for Brand Teams

This case study shows how a brand team can prepare its guidance, language, assets, and approvals for AI-assisted execution without losing control of meaning. The work focuses on turning brand knowledge that usually sits in decks, guidelines, and expert judgement into structured rules that machines can query and teams can govern. The outcome is a clearer operating model for AI readiness: brand intent becomes explicit, responsibilities become easier to assign, and future agents have a reliable source of policy before they generate or approve work.

AI systems require structured, explicit rules. This case study focuses on translating brand guidelines into machine-readable policies.
