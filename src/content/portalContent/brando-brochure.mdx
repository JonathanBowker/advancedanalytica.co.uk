# Brando brochure

Use this protected page when someone needs the short version first.

Brando turns brand knowledge into an AI-operated asset: structure, rules, approvals, and evidence that can govern outputs at the point content is created. It is designed for organisations that need speed without losing control.

## What this page is for

- Giving a client or partner a tighter first product explanation than the public site.
- Framing the conversation before a working session or diagnostic call.
- Moving from curiosity to a more serious discussion about governed AI operations.

## What Brando covers

- Structured brand knowledge that machines can retrieve and use.
- Approval and policy logic that can be checked at runtime.
- A governed operating asset that sits inside real workflows, not beside them.
- Evidence and traceability that help teams defend decisions under scrutiny.
