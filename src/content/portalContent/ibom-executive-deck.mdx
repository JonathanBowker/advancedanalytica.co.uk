# IBOM executive deck

This protected page works like a short presentation overview for leadership conversations.

The IBOM framework is the delivery and operating model that gets from opportunity to governed execution. It gives the client a clear path: scope, define, design, checkpoint, build, and ongoing evolution.

## What leaders need to see

- Where decisions are made and validated.
- How specification quality is checked before build begins.
- What expert supervision looks like in an AI-assisted delivery model.
- How the method reduces drift, rework, and governance surprises later.
