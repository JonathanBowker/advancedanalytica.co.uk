# IBOM technology briefing

IBOM is the Intelligent Business Operations Manager. It gives people and AI agents governed access to approved business data, knowledge, policies, tools, and workflows.

## What IBOM covers

- Structured business data and knowledge with ownership and provenance.
- Permissions, policies, approval rules, and operating boundaries.
- Governed access to tools, workflows, and agent capabilities.
- Evidence and traceability that support accountable decisions.
- Brand data, including colours, typography, imagery, language, assets, standards, and approvals.

## How Agent in the Room uses IBOM

IBOM provides approved business context for Agent in the Room. The live agent can speak from governed information while Advanced Analytica experts lead the workshop.
