# Your woodland is generating carbon credits. Is anyone capturing the evidence?

Woodland can generate long-term carbon value, but the value is only as strong as the evidence behind it.

The UK's Woodland Carbon Code is built around registration, carbon accounting, monitoring, validation, and verification. GOV.UK describes it as a scheme with a transparent UK Land Carbon Registry, independent validation and verification, and science-led prediction and monitoring of sequestration.

That means the commercial asset is not simply the woodland. It is the woodland plus the evidence system.

> A carbon credit is not a tree. It is a verified claim about a tree, over time.

## The evidence gap

Many estates already hold the raw material: planting plans, contractor invoices, compartment maps, drone imagery, site photographs, species mixes, maintenance records, grant documentation, fencing records, deer control notes, and inspection reports.

The problem is that the evidence is often scattered. It sits in emails, folders, paper files, phones, spreadsheets, consultant reports, and memories.

That is fragile. Verification does not reward effort that cannot be evidenced.

| Evidence type | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Registration record | Confirms the project entered the scheme at the right time |
| Boundary and compartment maps | Links carbon claims to defined land parcels |
| Planting and establishment records | Shows what was planted, where, and when |
| Monitoring data | Supports continuing claims about project progress |
| Photographic evidence | Creates a timestamped visual record |
| Validation and verification statements | Converts project claims into trusted market evidence |

## AI as the evidence layer

Agentic AI can support the operational burden by turning woodland carbon evidence into a live evidence pipeline.

An AI-driven system can watch for missing documents, structure incoming records, link evidence to compartments, draft progress reports, prepare verification packs, and alert the estate team long before a deadline becomes a problem.

It can also keep the audit trail intact. Every generated report should link back to the evidence that supports it. Every assumption should be visible. Every missing record should become an action, not a surprise.

## Why this is commercial

Woodland carbon is not just environmental work. For estates, it is a long-duration asset class. The asset needs governance because the value is realised through credibility.

If the evidence is weak, the claim is weak. If the claim is weak, the value is weak.

The estates that build structured evidence capture early will have a better position when buyers, auditors, lenders, planning authorities, or professional advisers ask the obvious question:

> Can you prove it?
