# IBOM is the Intelligent Business Operations Manager.

IBOM is Advanced Analytica's technology for governing the business data, knowledge, policies, tools, and workflows used by people and AI agents.

## Why it exists

Businesses already hold the information AI needs. It sits across systems, documents, assets, policies, workflows, specialists, and operational records.

Without a governed layer, agents can retrieve the wrong source, miss its context, or act beyond the boundaries set by the business.

IBOM brings every source the client makes available into one controlled environment. Ownership, provenance, permissions, status, and relationships stay attached.

## What IBOM does

- Structures approved business data and knowledge.
- Preserves the context and controls attached to each source.
- Supports exact retrieval and search by meaning.
- Gives agents governed access to approved tools and workflows.
- Connects outputs and actions to evidence, rules, and decision context.
- Keeps access and activity governed by the client's policies.

Brand information can sit within IBOM alongside other business data. That includes colours, typography, imagery, language, assets, standards, and approval rules.

## IBOM and Agent in the Room

IBOM provides governed business context for Agent in the Room. The live agent can speak from approved information while Advanced Analytica experts lead the workshop.

This gives the client's data and knowledge a voice without separating the conversation from its evidence, permissions, and rules.

<Insight>
IBOM is the governed technology. Agent in the Room brings its approved business context into a live, expert-led conversation.
</Insight>

## What IBOM is not

IBOM is not a generic chatbot, a loose automation layer, or an unstructured document store.

It doesn't replace business ownership or expert judgement. It gives people and agents a controlled way to use what the business knows.
