## How It Works

## Direction without inventory is malpractice.

We will not tell a business what to do with its AI until we know exactly what it already has, what is built, what is shadow IT, what is actually working, and what has simply been bought and left idle.

Every engagement starts with an agentic due diligence pass across the organisation's AI estate: technical, operational, and commercial. It is not a formality. It is the only honest way to know which decisions are actually needed.

### Agentic due diligence checklist

| Area | What we check |
| --- | --- |
| 01 | AI tools already approved, bought, trialled, or abandoned |
| 02 | Shadow AI use across teams, suppliers, and operational workflows |
| 03 | Data, policy, security, brand, compliance, and governance exposure |
| 04 | Existing automation, agentic systems, prompts, workflows, and assets |
| 05 | What is working, what is idle, and what should be stopped |

## Why Interim Rather Than Recruit

Interim leadership gives the business accountable direction now, while keeping the permanent decision properly informed.
